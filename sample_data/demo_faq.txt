# Demo client: "Harborview Coffee Co." (fictional)

## Hours
We are open Monday–Friday 7:00–18:00, Saturday 8:00–17:00. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Location
We are at 42 Marine Drive, Harborview. Street parking is available; the blue bay in front is 30 minutes only.

## Menu highlights
We serve single-origin pour-over, house espresso blend, cold brew on tap, and daily pastries from a local bakery. Dairy alternatives: oat and almond.

## Orders and waiting time
Pour-over takes about 4–6 minutes at busy times. Espresso drinks are usually 2–4 minutes. During the morning rush (8:00–10:00), expect an extra few minutes.

## Catering
We offer coffee catering for offices and small events (10–80 people). Lead time is at least 5 business days. Reply with date, headcount, and dietary needs for a quote.

## Refunds and issues
If an item was wrong or unsatisfactory, tell us within 24 hours and we will remake it or offer store credit. We do not refund card purchases to cash.

## Wi‑Fi
Guest Wi‑Fi password is posted at the counter and rotates monthly; ask a barista if it does not connect.

## Dogs
Well-behaved dogs on a leash are welcome on the patio only, not inside the café.

## Contact
Email: hello@harborviewcoffee.example — we usually reply within one business day.
